## 🌸 ✨ Key Features

### 🎵 Retro MP3 Player
* **Persistent Audio Playback**: Remembers your current track, volume, playback state, and exact song timestamp across page loads using `localStorage`.
* **Draggable Window**: Move your retro music player anywhere on your desktop with smooth delta movement.
* **Curated Playlist**: Switch seamlessly between cozy tracks from BTS, Enhypen, Red Velvet, ILLIT, Joe Hisaishi, and more!

### 🍵 Café Menu & Treat Summoning
* **Interactive Carousel**: Browse through a delightful pastel menu featuring angel cake, choco boba, matcha lattes, mochi donuts, and sweet tanghulu 🍰🧋🍵.
* **Persistent Draggable Treats**: Summon any treat right onto your desktop, drag them around freely, or delete them when you're done. Your layout stays saved even after refreshing!

### 🐈‍⬛ Kuroo the Alive Cat Mascot
* **State-Driven GIF Animations**: Watch Kuroo come to life with animations for idling, sitting, sleeping, eating, meowing, and roaming left or right.
* **Autonomous Behavior**: Kuroo wanders around the screen, takes naps (complete with `💤` reactions), and reacts dynamically to your clicks.
* **Companion Window & Sync Toggle**: Click on Kuroo to open his interactive companion window where you can feed him treats or pet him. Use the dock button to toggle Kuroo and his window together in perfect sync!

### ⚠️ Aesthetic 404 Error Pages
* **System Warning Windows**: Custom retro-styled error pages that match the café vibe when a link goes missing.
* **Hidden Easter Eggs**: Try dragging the error window away to uncover Kuroo's secret stash hidden right underneath! 🤫🐾

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Backend**: Node.js, Express
* **Templating Engine**: EJS
* **Frontend Styling**: Custom CSS, Retro Y2K UI Elements, Google Fonts
* **Scripting**: Modular Vanilla JavaScript (`drag.js`, `cafe-menu.js`, `kuroo.js`, `music-player.js`)
* **State Management**: Browser `localStorage` for window positions, audio state, and spawned café treats.
* **Hosting**: Render 🚀
